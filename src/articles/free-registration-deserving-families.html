At Rishta Trust we believe financial constraint should never be an obstacle in finding an honourable rishta. That is why registration is free for families who genuinely cannot afford a fee.

Who can apply

  • Orphans
  • Widows
  • Individuals and families facing financial hardship

How applications are reviewed

Every application for free registration is subject to verification and approval by our management. This keeps the programme fair and makes sure support reaches the families it is meant for.

How to apply

Message a consultant on WhatsApp through our Contact page, or visit one of our branches, and ask about free registration. Your consultant will explain what the review involves.

The same care, the same privacy

Families registered free of charge go through the same structured process as everyone else — a registration sitting, profile screening, consent-based matching and respectful family introductions. The same confidentiality commitments apply: records are kept offline, photographs are shared only with consent, and personal information is never sold.
