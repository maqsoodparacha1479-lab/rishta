Trust & privacy

Why we never publish candidate photos

How we store photographs, when they are shared, and the rights you keep over your information.

24 Sep 2026 · 3 min read

Many matrimonial services put photographs and profiles online for anyone to browse. We made a deliberate choice not to.

How photographs are handled

  • Photographs are stored securely offline at our registered offices.
  • They are never uploaded to public platforms.
  • A photograph is shared with another family only after your explicit prior consent.
  • Access is limited to authorized senior consultants.

What you see on our website

The proposal listings on our website show no names, no photographs and no contact details. When a family is interested, we speak to both sides first — details are shared only when both families agree.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, ask for it to be deleted (subject to legal obligations), or withdraw your consent to sharing at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.

A shared responsibility

Privacy works both ways. Families who receive proposal details or photographs agree not to share them with anyone else without consent.
