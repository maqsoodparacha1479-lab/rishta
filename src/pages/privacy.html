Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Rishta Trust ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients and their families. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our matrimonial and family matchmaking consultancy services.

By registering with Rishta Trust or using our website, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Name, age, gender, CNIC number, marital status, and family details.
  • Contact Information: Phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and residential address.
  • Educational & Professional: Degrees, certifications, profession, and employment details.
  • Photographs: Candidate photographs (stored securely offline and shared only with explicit consent).
  • Family References: Information provided by family members or guardians.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for:

  • Matching suitable proposals based on your preferences and values.
  • Verifying identity, education, and profession (with your authorization).
  • Coordinating family introductions and meetings.
  • Maintaining confidential records for follow-up and support.
  • Communicating updates regarding potential matches.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is shared only:

  • With a potential match's family, and only after you give explicit prior consent.
  • With authorized personnel for verification purposes (CNIC, degrees, etc.).
  • When required by law or legal authorities.

5. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your information:

  • All records are stored securely offline in our registered offices.
  • Photographs are never uploaded to public platforms or shared without consent.
  • Access to your data is limited to authorized senior consultants only.
  • We do not share your phone number or personal details with third parties.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Withdraw consent for data sharing at any time.

7. Cookies & Website

Our website may use basic cookies for functionality and analytics. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
