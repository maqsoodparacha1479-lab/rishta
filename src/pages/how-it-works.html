How it works

Five steps, with your family at every one

A clear, unhurried process. You always know what happens next, and nothing is shared without your agreement.

  1. 01

    Register

    Send your details through our registration form, on WhatsApp, or by visiting an office. A consultant contacts you to confirm them. The one-time registration fee is {{fee.amount}}, paid to your consultant with an official receipt.

  2. 02

    Build your profile

    A senior consultant sits with your family to understand background and expectations and completes the candidate information sheet. You sign a privacy consent form. Management screens the profile, and with your authorization we verify identity, degrees, profession and references.

  3. 03

    Discover matches

    Your consultant searches our private database for proposals that fit your family’s values and preferences. You can also browse sample listings and ask about any of them.

  4. 04

    Connect

    A proposal summary is shared only after both families give prior permission. The first family-to-family meeting is arranged at our office or an agreed venue.

  5. 05

    Take the next step

    We stay in touch through family deliberations up to Nikah, or whatever your family decides. There is never any pressure to accept a proposal.
