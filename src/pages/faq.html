Registration & fee

What is the registration fee? {{icon:plus}}

Registration is a one-time fee of {{fee.amount}}. You pay it to your consultant after they have confirmed your details, and you receive an official receipt. Please read the fee terms in our Terms & Conditions.

How does registration work? {{icon:plus}}

Send your details through the registration form (five short steps), on WhatsApp, or by visiting an office. A consultant contacts you to confirm your details and arranges a registration sitting with your family. Your profile is screened before matching begins.

Can I pay online? {{icon:plus}}

No. This website does not take payments. Your consultant explains the payment options after confirming your details. We will never ask for card details, OTP codes or passwords.

Can parents register on behalf of their son or daughter? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. Parents and guardians often register for a candidate. The candidate and the family members involved must consent to the registration, and the candidate must be at least 18.

Is there help for families who cannot afford the fee? {{icon:plus}}

Orphans, widows and families facing financial hardship can ask about free registration. It is subject to management verification and approval. Read more.

Privacy & safety

How are profiles protected? {{icon:plus}}

Records are stored securely offline at our offices and access is limited to authorized senior consultants. A proposal is shared with another family only after both families give permission, and personal information is never sold.

Will my photos be published online? {{icon:plus}}

No. Photographs are never uploaded to public platforms. They are shared with another family only after your explicit consent.

Do you verify profiles? {{icon:plus}}

Every profile is screened for completeness and realistic expectations. With your authorization we can also verify identity (CNIC), degrees, profession and family references.

What happens to the details I send through the registration form? {{icon:plus}}

The website does not store them. The form prepares a WhatsApp message to the consultant you choose, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

Matching

Do you help families living overseas? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. We work with families in the UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Canada, Australia and the United States, through our head office in Faisalabad or any branch.

Do you guarantee a marriage? {{icon:plus}}

No. Marriage is a decision for the families and individuals involved. We facilitate introductions honestly and never push proposals.

Why don’t listings show names or photos? {{icon:plus}}

To protect every candidate. Names, photos and contact details are shared privately by your consultant, only when both families agree.
