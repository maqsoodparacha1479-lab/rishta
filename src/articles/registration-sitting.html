Every registration with Rishta Trust starts with a structured sitting with one of our senior consultants. It is a conversation, not an interview — our aim is to understand your family and what you are genuinely looking for.

Who should attend

The candidate, a parent or guardian, or both. Guardians are welcome to register on a candidate’s behalf, but the candidate and the family members involved must consent to the registration. Candidates must be at least 18 years old.

What we will ask about

  • Personal and family background, and marital status
  • Education and profession
  • Family origin and current city — in Pakistan or overseas
  • Your preferences and realistic expectations for a match

These details go onto a candidate information sheet. Please share accurate, truthful information — misrepresentation can lead to services being ended.

The privacy consent form

You will be asked to sign a privacy consent form. It explains how your information is used and protected. You can read the same commitments in our Privacy Policy before you visit.

If you would like verification

Verification is optional and done only with your authorization. If you choose it, your consultant will tell you which documents to bring — for example identity (CNIC) and education or employment records.

Fees and receipts

Registration is a one-time fee of {{fee.amount}}. It covers record set-up and screening, and an official receipt is issued for every payment. Your consultant explains how to pay; payment is never taken on this website. Please read the fee terms in our Terms of Service, including when the fee becomes non-refundable.

What happens next

After the sitting, your profile is screened by management, verified if you chose that option, and then our consultants begin searching our private database for suitable proposals.
