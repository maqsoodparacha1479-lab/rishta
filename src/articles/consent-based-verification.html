Trust is the foundation of every good match. We build it in two layers: screening, which applies to every profile, and verification, which happens only with your authorization.

Screening — for every profile

Before any profile enters our private database, it is reviewed for completeness and realistic expectations. Profiles are never shared with another family before this review.

Verification — with your consent

With your authorization, we can verify:

  • Identity (CNIC)
  • Educational degrees
  • Professional status
  • Family references

Your consultant will explain what verification involves and which documents are needed before anything is checked.

Why consent matters

Verification involves sensitive documents. We only check what you authorize, and verification information is handled by authorized personnel only.

An honest note

Screening and verification reduce risk, but no process can guarantee every detail a client provides. We encourage every family to take part fully in introductions and make their own enquiries before any decision.
