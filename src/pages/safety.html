Meeting a proposal

Staying safe

Most introductions go smoothly. These simple steps help keep it that way.

  1. Meet with family. Hold first meetings at our office or a venue both families have agreed.
  2. Take your time. Ask your consultant any questions and never feel rushed into a decision.
  3. Never send money to a proposal or their family, whatever the reason given.
  4. Keep documents private. Don’t share ID documents, bank details or private photos with anyone you’ve been introduced to.
  5. Tell us if something feels wrong. Your consultant would always rather hear about it.
